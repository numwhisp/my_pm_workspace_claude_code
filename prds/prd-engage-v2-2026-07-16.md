# PRD Skeleton: Post-Week-1 Re-engagement

*Draft based on #product-engage Slack thread, Mon 7/13 (Marcus, Raj, Lena). Starting point for Thursday discussion — not a finished document.*

## Problem Statement

30-day retention has dropped to 37%, down from 44% last quarter.

- Churn is sharpest among users who don't set a savings goal in week 1 — almost double the churn rate of users who do (Raj).
- User research suggests people who connect their account and see the dashboard don't have a clear next step after the initial spending breakdown (Lena).
- The home feed doesn't change based on user activity — it looks the same whether a user last opened the app yesterday or three weeks ago (Raj).
- The weekly summary email has a 22% open rate, but the in-app experience doesn't continue the story the email starts, creating a jarring disconnect (Lena).

Working hypothesis: users go passive because the app stops feeling relevant after the first "aha moment," and there's nothing pulling them back with a reason specific to them.

- [To be defined Thursday — align on what we're solving before designing solutions, per Marcus]
- Directionally, based on thread: give users a reason to return to the app that's personalized to their own activity/patterns, not generic tips.
