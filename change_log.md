- Ran first usability round on the Engage v2 prototype. **What's working:** both screens were correctly understood at a glance (no confusion about function/purpose); the in-app screen rated "High" impact on actual spending behavior — peer benchmarking paired with single-tap actions ("Move $50 to savings") made the adjustment feel effortless, not punitive. **Top 2 friction points:** (1) the email only rated "Moderate" behavior-change impact because it functions as a passive alert with no concrete action preview — testers explicitly want a teaser of the suggested move directly in the email; (2) the "similar users" peer benchmark is undefined, risking a fairness/trust perception problem in the core insight mechanic. **Highest-priority fix:** added an actionable teaser to the email ("💡 This week's move: Move $50 to savings") between the stat row and the CTA. Reasoning: the email is the funnel entry point for exactly the dormant-user scenario this prototype targets, testers tied its lower impact rating directly to the missing action preview, and it's a low-effort copy/layout change vs. the benchmark-clarity or goal-preset fixes (deferred to next round). - Ran a full metric diagnosis on the retention decline and week-5 experiment — saved to `data/metric-diagnosis.md`. Built a metric tree for 30-day retention (day-7 as a gate, session frequency, actionable-screen engagement, week-1 goal-setting, nudge volume/rate, acquisition channel, weekly-summary exposure). Specific cause of the weeks 1-4 decline: session frequency dropped 36% cohort-over-cohort, concentrated in actionable screens (savings_goal/weekly_summary/notifications fell 42-50%) vs. home (-19%) — not a nudge-volume or acquisition-mix problem, both stayed flat. Counter-intuitive finding: within treatment, opening the summary doesn't distinguish churners from retained users (97% vs 94%, nearly identical), and churners acted on the nudge slightly more than retained users — a reversal worth flagging, not dismissing. Generated 4 ranked hypotheses for treatment churn (acquisition channel quality, platform/Android gap, goal-setting not a strong within-treatment discriminator, reactive over-engagement by churners) with confidence scores and confirm/rule-out data points for each. Recommended testing the platform/Android gap first — not highest-confidence, but the one Raj's team can act on this sprint via a notification-delivery audit.
- Ran a guided significance pressure-test and full experiment design on the week-5 pilot — saved to `data/experiment-design.md`. Finding that changes everything downstream: the pilot's 36% vs 22% result is **not statistically significant** (p≈0.124, 95% CI −3.6 to +31.6 points, includes zero) — n=50/arm cannot yet distinguish a real effect from a lucky cohort. A properly powered test needs ~1,160 users per variant (~2,320 total, ~23x the pilot). Test duration depends on an unresolved scoping decision: new-signups-only (matching the pilot design) needs ~27 weeks and does not fit an 8-week window; broadening to the full 85,000 WAU pool needs ~5 weeks and fits comfortably — a ~22-week swing that only Marcus/the team can decide, not a stats question. Recommendation: wait for the full test, do not scale on the pilot alone. Defined 3 weekly leading indicators to monitor during the test (day-7 gap, summary open-rate trend, iOS/Android split) so we're not blind for the full window.
