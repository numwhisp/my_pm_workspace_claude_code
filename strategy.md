# Strategy — Recovering the 7-Point Retention Drop

30-day retention: 44% → 37% over the last two quarters (source: Marcus, #product-engage, 2026-07-13). Gap to close: 7 points.

## Hypothesis

Users go passive because the app stops feeling relevant after the first "aha moment" (the initial spending breakdown). Nothing afterward gives them a reason to return that's personal, timely, or actionable.

## Supporting evidence (from #product-engage thread, 2026-07-13)

- Churn is sharpest among users who don't set a savings goal in week 1 — almost double the churn rate of users who do. (Raj)
- User research: people who connect an account and see the dashboard don't have a clear next step after the spending breakdown. (Lena)
- The home feed is static — identical whether the user was last active yesterday or three weeks ago. (Raj)
- The weekly summary email gets a 22% open rate, but the in-app experience doesn't continue what the email started. (Lena)

**Assumption, not yet validated:** that fixing post-week-1 relevance (vs. other factors — e.g., pricing, competitor switching, unrelated app-quality issues) is sufficient to close the gap. Not tested against alternative causes yet.

## Direction under exploration

In-app personalized weekly summary screen (concept: Lena) — top insight for the week, one actionable nudge based on the user's own patterns, savings-goal progress. Raj confirmed technically feasible on existing data; ranking logic for insight selection still needs to be built.

**Not yet decided.** Marcus wants problem alignment before solutioning (Thursday). This is a direction being explored, not a committed plan.
