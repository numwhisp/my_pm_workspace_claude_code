# Nudge — Engage v2: Project Overview

## What Nudge is

Consumer personal finance app. Users connect bank accounts, see a spending breakdown, set savings goals, and get nudges to stay on track.

- Launched 4 years ago. Series B funded, $42M raised.
- 2.1M registered users, 340K MAU, growing 28% YoY on MAU.
- Core acquisition funnel (signup → connect account → spending breakdown) works well; the problem is what happens after activation.

## My squad: Engage

Owns everything related to keeping users active after they connect their first account.

- Surfaces: home feed, weekly money summaries, savings nudges, push notifications.
- Triad: Raj (Senior Engineer), Lena (Product Designer), me (PM).
